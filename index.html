A Brief Introduction

About me.

I'm an 18 year old, Alevels student based in Karachi, Pakistan. I enjoy experimenting with various forms of art. I love learning new skills and improving myself to the best version of me! I have been told I have something called an Overactive Imagination and discovering media studies became the creative outlet for bringing my creativity out in a completely new way, which was ofcourse through visual storytelling. And that is how Filmmaking has become my biggest interest now, and I beleive my fate lies in making stories that can change how we as a society interact with media and how this medium can change mindsets. I aspire to write my own novels and eventually adapt them into films, allowing me to translate my creative vision into films as I envision them in my head. .
